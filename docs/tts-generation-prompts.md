# TTS Generation Prompts

Use these prompts to generate the 25 listening audio assets in `docs/media-assets.md`. Save the resulting WAV files under the exact paths shown below.

Voice direction: warm, clear ESL teacher voice for A1-A2; friendly mixed teacher/character voice for B1; natural adult situational voice for B2-C1. Use a neutral accent, moderate pace, clean audio, no music, and no sound effects. Keep each clip between 18 and 28 seconds.

## A1 Audio

### `seed/a1/listening-1.wav`
TTS script: Hello! I am someone you say when you meet a person. I can be short, like "Hi," or friendly, like "Good morning." I help people start talking. What am I?

### `seed/a1/listening-2.wav`
TTS script: Listen carefully. Open your book, write your name, and look at the board. These words tell students what to do in class. Do you do the action now or later? What am I?

### `seed/a1/listening-3.wav`
TTS script: I am at a small snack counter. I say, "Can I have a juice and a sandwich, please?" I use polite words when I want food. What am I doing?

### `seed/a1/listening-4.wav`
TTS script: My day has times. I go to school at eight. I eat lunch at twelve. I do homework at five. Are these times random, or are they part of my day?

### `seed/a1/listening-5.wav`
TTS script: Hi, my name is Leo. I am from Brazil. I have one sister, and I like soccer. I am telling you simple information about me. What am I doing?

## A2 Audio

### `seed/a2/listening-1.wav`
TTS script: This weekend, I am going to visit my cousin. I will take the bus at nine o'clock and arrive before lunch. Is this about the past or a future travel plan?

### `seed/a2/listening-2.wav`
TTS script: I do this every Saturday. I need a pencil, paper, and quiet time. I like it because I can create pictures. I am not playing music or sports. What is my hobby?

### `seed/a2/listening-3.wav`
TTS script: Today will be cloudy in the morning and rainy in the afternoon. Take an umbrella if you go outside. Is the speaker talking about clothes, food, or the weather?

### `seed/a2/listening-4.wav`
TTS script: Excuse me, how much is this blue jacket? Do you have it in a smaller size? I like it, but I want to check the price first. Where am I?

### `seed/a2/listening-5.wav`
TTS script: I have a sore throat and a headache. The doctor says I should drink water and rest today. Is the problem about health, shopping, or travel?

## B1 Audio

### `seed/b1/listening-1.wav`
TTS script: I finished the first part of the project, but I still need to check the numbers. I will send the update tomorrow morning. What is the worker explaining: progress, a vacation, or a complaint?

### `seed/b1/listening-2.wav`
TTS script: This app helps me organize homework, messages, and deadlines in one place. I use it when I study with classmates online. What problem does the technology solve?

### `seed/b1/listening-3.wav`
TTS script: If we bring reusable bottles and sort our trash, we can reduce waste at school. Is this advice about saving money, helping the environment, or planning a party?

### `seed/b1/listening-4.wav`
TTS script: Attention, students. The science fair starts at two o'clock in Room 12. Please bring your project poster and arrive ten minutes early. What important details did you hear?

### `seed/b1/listening-5.wav`
TTS script: Last year, I missed my bus on the first day of school. I felt nervous, but a teacher helped me find the office. What was the problem, and what was the result?

## B2 Audio

### `seed/b2/listening-1.wav`
TTS script: We are on schedule with the design, but the client changed one requirement. We can deliver on Friday if we simplify the final report. What tradeoff is the speaker suggesting?

### `seed/b2/listening-2.wav`
TTS script: Some people say short videos help students learn quickly. Others think they reduce attention span. Which two opinions are being compared, and where do you stand?

### `seed/b2/listening-3.wav`
TTS script: The exhibition was visually impressive, but the guide did not explain the cultural background clearly. What is the speaker praising, and what are they criticizing?

### `seed/b2/listening-4.wav`
TTS script: Before buying something expensive, compare prices, check your monthly budget, and leave space for emergencies. Which action helps avoid financial stress?

### `seed/b2/listening-5.wav`
TTS script: A productive routine is not only about working harder. It also includes sleep, movement, and time away from screens. What idea about wellbeing is the speaker emphasizing?

## C1 Audio

### `seed/c1/listening-1.wav`
TTS script: The main risk is not the technology itself, but the timeline. If we delay testing, adoption will suffer. What recommendation is implied by this briefing?

### `seed/c1/listening-2.wav`
TTS script: One manager wants the fastest solution; another argues that fairness matters more, even if it takes longer. What ethical tension is being discussed?

### `seed/c1/listening-3.wav`
TTS script: The product is promising because it solves a real user problem, but we still need evidence before scaling it. What benefit and uncertainty does the pitch include?

### `seed/c1/listening-4.wav`
TTS script: The issue is global, but its effects are local: schools, families, and workers experience it differently. What nuance does the speaker add to the discussion?

### `seed/c1/listening-5.wav`
TTS script: We cannot prioritize speed, cost, and quality equally. If quality is non-negotiable, we must either extend the timeline or reduce scope. What strategic choice is presented?
